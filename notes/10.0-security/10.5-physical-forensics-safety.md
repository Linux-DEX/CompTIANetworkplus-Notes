# 10.5 Physical Security, Forensics, and Safety

Section **10.0 Security**.

## Physical controls

A packet filter does not matter if the server is carried out of the building.

| Control | What it actually stops |
|---|---|
| Locked closet | A stranger plugging into a switch or walking off with a firewall |
| Badge access, unique to a person | Shared PIN pads. You cannot tell who entered if everyone knows 1234 |
| Mantrap or a turnstile | Tailgating. One badge, one person |
| Camera | A record after the fact. It does not stop the event unless someone is watching |
| Rack lock and port locks | Opportunistic unplugging. Not a determined person with time |
| Screen privacy and a locked session | A visitor at a desk reading a ticket that contains a password |
| Asset tags and a inventory | You notice the missing switch. You cannot notice what you never listed |
| Destruction of disks | A discarded drive is still a drive. Wipe or shred. Deleting the files is not wiping |
| Environmental | Fire suppression that will not destroy the equipment you are saving, temperature, a water sensor under the raised floor |

**Tailgating** is following an authorized person through a door they opened. The mitigation is the door, the training ("you may be rude to the person behind you"), and a culture where challenging someone is normal.

**Shoulder surfing** is reading a screen or a keypad by being nearby. **Dumpster diving** is reading what you threw away. Both are physical access to information. Shredding and screen locks are the controls.

## Forensics, the constraints

When an incident may become an investigation, the order is: keep people safe, keep the business running as well as you must, and **do not destroy the only copy of the evidence while you clean up**.

| Practice | Why |
|---|---|
| Note the time, from a reliable clock | A timeline built from three unsynchronized devices is an argument |
| Image the disk, work on the image | You get one chance at the original. A hash of the image shows it was not edited later |
| Do not power a machine off casually, and do not keep using it | Memory holds process lists and keys that disappear on power loss. Continued use overwrites logs |
| Chain of custody | Who had the drive, when, and where it was stored. A drive that sat on a desk is hard to rely on later |
| Capture volatile data first if it matters | RAM, established connections, logged-in users. Then the disk |
| Write-block when you attach a drive | Mounting a disk normally writes to it |

This is a specialist's job past the first hour. Your part is not to reimage the only infected machine before anyone has copied it, and not to log in as the user and click around.

Legal hold and who you call (management, counsel, law enforcement) are organizational decisions. Know whose number that is before you need it.

## Safety

| Hazard | Practice |
|---|---|
| Fiber | Do not look into the end of a live fiber. The wavelength is invisible and it damages an eye. Cap every connector. Keep cut scraps off the bench and out of your fingers. They are glass |
| Electricity | A switch chassis and a UPS are not toys. One hand, the cover on, and a lockout if you are in a panel you are not qualified to open. PoE is low energy compared with mains and it can still startle you off a ladder |
| Ladders and ceilings | Two points of contact. Do not stand on the top step. Know what else is in the ceiling besides your cable |
| Weight | A UPS and a full switch have injured more installers than malware has. Use two people |
| Fire | Know the extinguisher type. Do not spray a random extinguisher into a running rack. Know the exit |
| ESD | A strap when you are handling boards. Less dramatic than the others, and how NICs die on a dry day |
| Chemicals | Fiber cleaner and solvent are not for your skin. Read the label in the closet before you need it |

Grounding is both a safety path and a noise path (1.8). Do not lift a ground to make a hum go away. Find the loop instead.

The network is allowed to stay down while someone gets out of a ceiling and off a ladder. Availability does not outrank that.
