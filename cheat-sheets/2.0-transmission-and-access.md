# 2.0 Transmission and Access — cheat sheet

## 2.1 How bits move

- Simplex one way. Half duplex both ways, one at a time (hub, Wi-Fi). Full duplex both at once (switched Ethernet).
- Serial = one bit after another. Parallel = several wires, skew kills long runs.
- Synchronous = shared clock, bulk data. Asynchronous = start and stop bit per byte (RS-232).
- Baseband = entire medium, one signal, the BASE in 100BASE-TX. Broadband = frequency channels, cable TV.
- Several baseband talks share by time (TDM). Several broadband talks share by frequency (FDM).
- BPL = data on power wiring. Niche. Transformers and radio interference are the problems.

## 2.2 Who may transmit

- CSMA/CD (half-duplex Ethernet): listen, send, if collision then jam, random backoff, grow the wait. Not used on Wi-Fi. Not used on full-duplex switch ports.
- CSMA/CA (802.11): listen, wait DIFS, random backoff, send, require an ACK. RTS/CTS reserves the channel against hidden nodes.
- Collision domain: who can collide with me. Hub = 1. Switch = 1 per port.
- Broadcast domain: who hears a broadcast. Switch forwards it. Router and VLAN boundary stop it.
- Token = only the holder sends. Polling = master asks each station. Demand priority = station requests a turn.
- Mux combines, demux splits. TDM time, STDM time but only if there is data, FDM frequency, WDM colors on fiber.

## 2.3 Signaling

- Analog = continuous wave, noise accumulates. Digital = two levels, regenerate a clean bit.
- NRZ: level is the bit, long runs lose the clock. NRZI: a 1 is a transition. Manchester: transition in the middle of every bit (10BASE-T), costs bandwidth.
- Modem = digital device onto an analog channel. Codec = analog source into digital, or compression. ADC in, DAC out.
- ASK amplitude, FSK frequency, BPSK one bit by phase, QPSK two bits, QAM amplitude + phase (many bits). Higher QAM needs a cleaner signal.
- Differential = compare the pair, noise cancels. Single-ended = compare to ground.
- Line rate is bits. 1 byte = 8 bits. 1 Gb/s ≈ 125 MB/s before overhead. Mb ≠ MB.
