# 10.0 Security — cheat sheet

## Basics

- Confidentiality, integrity, availability. Least privilege. Defense in depth. Separation of duties.
- MFA = two different factors. A second password is not a factor. 802.1X = no traffic until RADIUS says yes.
- Firewall = policy, stateful if it tracks the connection. ACL = first match, implicit deny at the bottom.
- IDS = a copy, alerts only. IPS = inline, can drop. Fail open keeps traffic and stops inspecting. Fail closed does the opposite.
- Change default passwords and SNMP communities. Patch. Ship logs off the box.

## Crypto and IPSec

- Symmetric (AES) = one key, fast. Asymmetric = sign and exchange that key. Hash (SHA-256) is not encryption. MD5 is retired.
- A certificate binds a name to a public key. TLS checks CA, name, and expiry. Clicking through the warning turns the check off.
- ESP encrypts. AH does not. Tunnel mode = site-to-site. IKE UDP 500. NAT-T UDP 4500. IKEv2.
- Do not NAT packets that belong in the tunnel.

## Attacks, by effect

- DoS fills bandwidth, half-open connections, or expensive requests. A full circuit has to be filtered upstream of you.
- Spoofing pretends. ARP spoofing is local (DAI). Rogue DHCP is local (DHCP snooping). Evil twin = your SSID, their AP (802.1X).
- Phishing asks the user. MFA means the password they just typed is not enough.
- VLAN hopping dies when access ports cannot become trunks and the native VLAN carries no users.

## Wireless and policy

- WEP is broken. WPA2-Personal = one passphrase. WPA2-Enterprise = 802.1X. WPA3-Personal = SAE.
- Guest SSID = own VLAN, Internet only. Hiding the SSID is not a control.
- A policy names an owner. Logs need a retention time. Lost-laptop revocation needs a person to call.

## Physical, evidence, safety

- Unique badges, locked closets, cameras as a record, shred disks, do not share the keypad code.
- Image the disk, hash the image, write down custody. Do not reimage the only copy, and do not keep using it.
- Do not look into fiber. Do not lift a ground. Two people for a UPS.
